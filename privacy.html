Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hamaam Calculator mobile application developed by Yasir Hamid. The application is a simple construction calculation tool used for measuring and calculating Hamaam, marble, and stone construction work.

App Features

The app provides the following services:

  • Calculate construction measurements
  • Convert inch measurements to square feet
  • Calculate total cost based on rate
  • Generate measurement results
  • Allow users to print or save calculated measurements
  • Works offline without requiring internet connection

Information Collection

Hamaam Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. Users are not required to create accounts or provide personal information. All calculations are performed locally on the user’s device.

Internet Usage

The application does not require internet connection to function. All features are available offline.

Data Sharing

We do not collect or share any personal data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

This application does not collect any personal information from users, including children under the age of 13.
