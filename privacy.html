Privacy Policy

AH-Hamaam Construction Calculator ("we", "our", "app") is a construction utility application designed to calculate hamaam, marble, and stone work measurements and costs.

Information We Collect

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal user information. All calculations are performed locally on the user’s device.

Advertisements

The app may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device-related information such as device ID, advertising ID, and usage data according to Google’s Privacy Policy.

Internet Usage

Internet connection is required only for loading advertisements. The app works offline for calculation features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user personal data.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
